What is the Yellow Box Method?

•The Yellow Box Method is one of the core tools in the Fractal System, and it’s designedto help you understand where price is likely to stay, break out, or reverse — basedpurely on structure and probability.•

What Is the Yellow Box?

•The Yellow Box is the core balance zone — it’s the area where price tends to stayaround 80% to 90% of the time. This isn’t just a visual box on your chart — it’s whereliquidity sits, where most market activity takes place, and where you’ll see support,resistance, and consolidation happen naturally. It isn't random; instead, it's calculatedbased on market volatility and trader positioning, marking a zone of liquidity andbalance. As a trader, you want to think of the Yellow Box as the "fair value zone." Pricewill keep gravitating toward it unless something significant forces it out. It helps youfocus on what price is most likely to do next — not what you hope or fear it will do.•

Key Components of the Yellow Box Method

1.

Yellow Box (Core Balance Zone)

•

Definition:

This is the central area marked by upper and lower pricelevels, derived from historical volatility. Most trading activity occurs withinthis area.•

Importance:

Price tends to consolidate and revert within this zone, actingas the foundational area for day-to-day trading activities.2.

Edges of the Yellow Box

•These serve as critical "break points."•

Upper Edge:

Acts as resistance. If price breaks and holds above, itindicates potential bullish continuation.•

Lower Edge:

Serves as support. If price breaks and remains below, itsuggests bearish continuation.3.

Average Range Levels

•

Above the Yellow Box:

Represents resistance areas where price oftenmeets selling pressure.•

Below the Yellow Box:

Acts as support areas where price commonlyfinds buying interest.•These levels provide strong reversion or breakout opportunities.4.

Maximum Range Levels

•Defined as extreme price levels based on historical volatility data,indicating potential turning points during high volatility or news-drivenevents.•

What About the Red and Green Boxes?

•Alongside the Yellow Box, you'll also notice Red and Green Boxes on your chart. Theseare extensions beyond the core balance zone: The Red Box sits above the Yellow Boxand represents the average range high. If price breaks out of the Yellow Box to theupside, the Red Box becomes the likely target. It's often used to spot profit-takinglevels or resistance zones. The Green Box sits below the Yellow Box and marks theaverage range low. If price breaks down below the Yellow Box, the Green Box iswhere you should expect price to gravitate next. It’s often seen as a potential supportor reversal area. These zones give you clear targets and help you avoid chasing pricein the middle of nowhere.•How to Use It When price is inside the Yellow Box:•Expect range-bound movement. Focus on reversals from the top or bottom of the box.Use it for quick trims or scalps unless there's a clean breakout.•When price is breaking out of the Yellow Box:•That’s where things get interesting. A clean candle close outside the Yellow Box is yourfirst clue. If price closes above, it’s trying to seek the next level up — often targetingthe top of the average range (Red Box). If price closes below, it’s targeting the bottomof the average range (Green Box). But here’s the catch — not all breakouts are clean. Ifprice steps outside the box and then comes right back in without a solid close, it’soften a false breakout. You don’t want to chase that. Wait for confirmation.•Key Details to Watch For Candle Closes:•A close above or below the Yellow Box increases the win rate dramatically. A 1-minuteor 5-minute close adds real confirmation. Rejection at the Edge: If price hits the edgeof the box and can’t push through, expect a bounce right back to the opposite side.Average Range: Once outside the Yellow Box, price usually targets either the top (RedBox) or bottom (Green Box) of the average range before deciding what’s next.Reversion: If price doesn’t find strength past the average range, it’ll often revert backto the Yellow Box.

How to Use the Yellow Box Method

Step-by-Step Process:

1.

Identifying Daily Levels

•Each day after market close (typically around 5 PM EST), use your chartingsoftware with the "Option Analyzer" and select "Wave 7, 60-minute,50-day" to identify the current market close price and derive the YellowBox ranges for the following day.2.

Setting Up Your Charts

•Mark the upper and lower bounds of your Yellow Box clearly on yourtrading chart.•Identify and mark the average range and maximum range support andresistance zones.3.

Executing Trades

•

Breakouts:

Look for price action clearly breaking out of the Yellow Box.Confirm breakouts with candle closes (5-minute or 1-minute candles).•If a breakout occurs above the Yellow Box, target the upperaverage range level as your first profit target.•If a breakout occurs below, aim for the lower average range as theinitial profit target.•

Reversions:

When price hits the average range levels but fails to sustainmomentum, look for trades that revert back toward the Yellow Box.4.

Risk Management

•Always place your stop-loss just beyond the opposite edge of the YellowBox from your trade entry. This clearly defines your risk and keeps itmanageable.•Adjust your stops into profit once the market moves in your favor toprotect gains.5.

Trade Management

•Consider taking partial profits at your initial targets and letting theremaining positions run with trailing stops to capture potential largermoves.•On extreme volatility days, use maximum range levels as secondarytargets.•

How this helps you:

•This helps you by Instead of guessing, you’re reacting to structure. You know whenyou’re inside balance, when you’re leaving it, and when price is likely to reverse. It alsohelps with: Planning entries and exits Knowing where to trim profits Avoiding impulsivetrades The Yellow Box isn’t just about entries — it’s about context. When price is inbalance, let it play out. When it breaks? Be ready — but don’t jump in blindly. Letstructure guide your execution. Mastering this one piece of the system can improveyour win rate, reduce noise, and give you a cleaner roadmap every day you trade

Practical Example (Daily Trading Routine)

•After market close, record your Yellow Box and range levels take notes also and keeptrack.•Before market opens, clearly mark these levels on your charts.• Wait for clear signals:•Don’t forget if price breaks above the Yellow Box, place a long trade targetingthe upper average range.•if price breaks below, place a short trade targeting the lower average range.•Monitor closely, adjusting stops into profit as the trade moves in your favor.

Summary

By following the Yellow Box method, you're trading with a clear, structured approach basedon proven volatility data and historical market behavior. This method removes emotionaltrading, reduces guesswork, and boosts confidence by clearly defining high-probability priceranges and decision points each trading day.
